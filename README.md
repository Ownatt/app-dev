# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Introduction
My favorite series is **Stranger Things**. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers.

## About the Series
The story takes place in the fictional town of Hawkins, where a group of friends discovers mysterious events and a dangerous alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.

### My Favorite Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers

### Why I Like This Series
I like this series because:
1. The story is exciting and mysterious.
2. The characters have strong friendships.
3. The episodes have unexpected twists.
